Privacy Policy

Your hair. Your data.

Effective 9 October 2026 · Applies to the StrandWise app for Android

StrandWise has no ads and no account. We do not collect your personal information — everything you enter stays on your phone, and the app works fully offline.

1. What StrandWise is

StrandWise is a personal hair care coach: a weekly routine, an ingredient label scanner, a hair fall and photo tracker, and learning content. It is provided by the developer listed on its Google Play page.

2. Information we collect

None. StrandWise does not have user accounts, does not run its own servers for your data, and does not include advertising or analytics of its own. We never receive your answers, your photos, your hair fall counts or the labels you scan.

3. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your phone:

  • Your hair quiz answers (hair type, scalp, porosity, water, concerns) and your chosen language.
  • Your routine progress, streaks and reminder settings.
  • Your hair fall counts and notes.
  • Progress photos you choose to add.

This data is never uploaded by the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling StrandWise.

4. Camera, photos and the ingredient scanner

When you scan a label or add a progress photo, StrandWise asks Android to open your camera or photo gallery. Text on a label is read on your device using Google ML Kit text recognition. The picture and the text are not sent to us.

5. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app uses Android notifications, the vibration setting and a start-up permission so reminders can be rescheduled after your phone restarts. Reminders are created and delivered on your phone.

6. Third-party components

StrandWise is built with Flutter and uses Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition. Like other Google libraries, ML Kit may send limited technical diagnostics to Google, as described in Google’s ML Kit terms. These do not include the images or text you scan. Google Play may also collect information when you download or purchase the app, under Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. Children

StrandWise is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

8. Not medical advice

StrandWise gives general hair care information. It cannot diagnose or cure any condition. If you have concerns about hair loss or your scalp, please speak to a doctor.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

10. Contact

For any privacy question, please use the developer contact details shown on the StrandWise page on Google Play.
